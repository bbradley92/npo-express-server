Types of Grants We Pursue:

Programmatic

Purpose: A "programmatic grant" refers to funding for a specific program or project, rather than for general operational support.

Strategy: It is typically tied to specific goals, outcomes, and a defined budget, allowing funders to measure the direct impact of the grant on the program's beneficiaries.

General Operational Support:

Purpose: Unrestricted funding given to non-profit organizations to support their day-to-day operations and overall mission.

Goal: Unlike project-specific grants, GOS allows organizations to use the funds flexibly, addressing various operational needs and enhancing their capacity.

Capital Grant Support

Purpose: Capital grants support major investments in facilities, equipment, infrastructure, vehicles, accessibility improvements, renovations, construction, adaptive technology, and other long-term organizational assets.

Strategy: These grants require a clear case for need, a defined project budget, strong implementation planning, and a direct connection between the investment and community impact. NPO Grant Writing helps organizations frame capital projects in a way that is specific, fundable, and aligned with public, private, and foundation funding priorities.
