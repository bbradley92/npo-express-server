About NPO Grant Writing

Grant writing directed by strategy, evidence, and human judgment.

Brendan Bradley directs each engagement and developed the Grant Growth System™ after seeing how much grant work happens before and after the narrative itself.

Why GGS was developed

The narrative is only one part of the work.

Every proposal depends on current facts, the correct applicant entity, reliable source documents, live funder requirements, realistic request strategy, internal approval, exact transmitted files, and the obligations that follow an award.

NPO Grant Writing’s proprietary Grant Growth System™ brings those responsibilities into one human directed operating model so an organization can pursue more qualified opportunities without losing control of accuracy, relationships, or authority.

Founder and Grant Strategist

His work focuses on human services, disability services, behavioral health, education, housing, and community based organizations. He leads grant strategy, prospect research, qualification, institutional knowledge, document reconciliation, proposal development, portfolio design, and final professional review.

How NPO works

The operating principles are straightforward.

01

Verify before representing.

Material facts and live funder requirements are checked against the strongest available source.

02

Qualify before producing.

Eligibility, fit, access, request strategy, relationship history, and workload are assessed before substantial drafting.

03

Keep authority human.

Clients control their facts, commitments, relationships, approval, and final submission authority.

04

Measure the whole portfolio.

Production, awards, renewals, internal effort, reporting, and risks prevented all matter.
